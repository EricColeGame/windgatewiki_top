### What to Confirm Before You Sign

Every community is a little different, and policies change. Before signing a lease, confirm:

- Pet policy, breed restrictions, and any associated fees
- Whether utilities are billed separately or bundled
- Maintenance response times and how requests are submitted
- Lease term options and renewal terms
- Guest and additional-vehicle parking rules
- Whether lawn care is included or resident-managed

Getting these answers in writing prevents the kind of surprises that turn a good move into a stressful one.

## How to Evaluate Any Manufactured Home Community

Windgate is one option among several in the region, so it pays to compare systematically rather than emotionally. Use this checklist when you tour.

1. **Visit at two different times of day.** Morning and evening traffic tell you more than a scheduled showing.
2. **Drive the route to work.** Test the actual commute during rush hour, not on a Sunday.
3. **Ask about total monthly cost.** Rent plus utilities plus any fees is the real number.
4. **Check the home's condition firsthand.** Open cabinets, test water pressure, look at ceilings.
5. **Talk to a current resident.** A five-minute conversation beats a brochure.
6. **Read the lease carefully.** Understand renewal terms, notice periods, and maintenance responsibilities.
7. **Confirm what is included.** Lawn care, trash, and parking policies vary by community.

Doing this homework takes an afternoon and can save you a year of frustration.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Windgate Community

**Is the Windgate community a good fit for families with children?**

**Do residents own or rent their homes at Windgate?**
Windgate operates as a rental community, so residents lease rather than purchase. That means maintenance is handled by management and you avoid the costs of homeownership, including property taxes and major repairs.

**What amenities does the Windgate community offer?**
The community's primary listed amenity is off-street parking, with private parking situated just outside each home. Individual homes may also include updated kitchens, walk-in closets, carpeting, faux hardwood floors, and abundant windows. Amenities can vary by unit, so verify the specifics of the home you are considering.

**How does the Windgate community compare to renting an apartment nearby?**
The biggest differences are privacy and space. You get a detached home instead of shared walls, private parking instead of a shared lot, and more storage. Apartments may offer closer proximity to commercial districts, but for renters who value quiet and room to spread out, a manufactured home community often wins.

**Is New Braunfels a practical place to live if I work in San Antonio or Austin?**
Many residents commute to both. San Antonio is roughly 30 miles away and Austin is roughly 50 miles away, so commute times depend heavily on your specific workplace location and schedule. Testing the drive yourself during peak hours is the only reliable way to judge it.
