## Selling Windgate Wood and Avoiding Costly Mistakes

Selling is where most players quietly lose money. The payout itself is set by the game, not by other players, so the only variable you control is how efficiently you deliver logs. Treat this Windgate wood guide as a checklist and run through it before every session.

| Mistake | What It Costs You | The Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Selling one log at a time | Wasted minutes per run | Bank a full load, then sell |
| Overloading a small truck | Logs spill on turns and hills | Match the vehicle to the payload |
| Dropping logs far from the sell point | Extra hauling with no payout | Park close and unload in one motion |
| Chopping a tree that has not respawned | An empty trip | Scout first, chop second |
| Trading logs with strangers | Scams and lost inventory | Use the in-game sell point only |

If you play in a group, split roles. One player chops while another shuttles logs to the sell point. A two-person crew can roughly double the output of a solo run, because the chopper never stops swinging to drive.

## Pro Tips from Player Experience

These come from community reports and long-time player habits rather than any official manual:

- **Upgrade the axe before the truck.** Chopping speed gates everything else, and a slow axe turns a ten-minute run into thirty.
- **Pick low-population servers.** Fewer players means less lag, fewer collisions, and a clearer route.
- **Mark your route mentally, not physically.** Learn the drive so you are not hunting for the tree every session.
- **Keep a small reserve.** A handful of Windgate logs on hand saves a return trip when a build or trade request appears.
- **Log out with your load sold.** Inventory left in the world can be lost between sessions.
- **Watch for update notes.** Wood values, spawn behavior, and map layouts get adjusted over time, and yesterday's route may not be today's.

## Windgate Wood FAQ

**What is Windgate wood in Lumber Tycoon 2?**
Windgate is a specialty wood type that players chop, haul, and sell for money. It is generally regarded as a step above the starter woods, which makes it a natural target once you own a decent axe and a truck with real cargo space.

**Is Windgate wood worth the trip?**
For most players with mid-game gear, yes. The longer drive is offset by a higher payout per log and larger payloads per tree. If you are still using a starter axe and a tiny vehicle, farm closer woods first and come back when you have upgraded.

**What axe and vehicle do I need for Windgate wood?**
There is no single required setup, but community experience points to an upgraded axe and a vehicle with a large bed or trailer. Anything smaller turns a profitable run into a frustrating one.

**Can I sell Windgate wood to other players for more money?**
The in-game sell point is the safe option, and player-to-player trading is where most scams happen. Stick to the official sell point unless you fully trust the person you are trading with.

**Why does this Windgate wood guide mention values so generally?**
Because Lumber Tycoon 2 is updated regularly, and specific payouts change between versions. Focus on the process — scout, chop efficiently, haul in bulk, sell in one trip — and the numbers will follow whatever the current build supports.
