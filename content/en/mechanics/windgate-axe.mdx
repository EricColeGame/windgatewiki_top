## Trading the Windgate Axe Without Getting Burned

Trading is where the Windgate axe generates the most drama. Because the tool is scarce and its official documentation is thin, both buyers and sellers operate on rumor. That asymmetry is exactly what scammers exploit.

| Value Factor | Raises Price | Lowers Price |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed availability status | Currently unobtainable | Still farmable |
| Community documentation | Widely recognized | Disputed or unclear |
| Trade history | Multiple verified trades | No public record |
| Condition or duplication | Clean, single instance | Duplicated or flagged |
| Timing | Post-event scarcity | Mid-event flood |

Practical safeguards:

- **Use middlemen for high-value trades.** Reputable community middlemen exist for a reason.
- **Record the trade.** A screen capture settles disputes that chat logs cannot.
- **Distrust urgency.** "Last one, going fast" is a pressure tactic, not a market signal.
- **Price against multiple offers.** One quote is an anecdote; three is a market.

If you are buying the Windgate axe purely as an investment, remember that limited-tool markets are sentiment-driven. A single re-release can erase a premium overnight.
