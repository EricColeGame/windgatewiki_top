## The Windgate Tools Checklist: What to Bring (and What to Leave Behind)

Windgate tools aren't a single item — they're a loadout. The players who finish runs efficiently tend to treat their inventory the way a tradesperson treats a toolbox: everything in it has a job, and nothing in it is dead weight.

| Priority | Tool or supply | Why it earns a slot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | Primary axe | Cutting speed decides how much you bring home | Bring your best, not your favorite-looking |
| Essential | Backup axe | A swap or mistake shouldn't end the trip | Keep it in a known slot |
| High | Vehicle | Log transport and faster repositioning | Match the vehicle to the load size |
| High | Lighting / visibility aid | Navigation in shaded or tight areas | Especially useful on first visits |
| Medium | Empty inventory slots | Room for the actual haul | Clear space before you leave |
| Medium | Cash reserve | Covers small mid-run needs | Don't travel with everything you own |
| Low | Route reference | Keeps you from re-learning the path | A second screen or notes work fine |

A few loadout principles that hold up regardless of game version:

- **Bring your best axe, not your rarest one.** Value doesn't cut wood; speed and reliability do.
- **Never carry your entire net worth.** Community reports are full of players who lost a fortune because they packed everything "just in case."
- **Empty slots are a tool.** Every filler item you carry is a log you can't bring back.
- **Test your loadout locally first.** Do a short run near your base with the exact Windgate tools you plan to take. If something feels awkward at home, it will feel worse on a long trip.

If you're unsure whether an item belongs, ask one question: *does this help me get there, cut faster, carry more, or get back safely?* If the answer is no, it stays home.

## Solo vs. Co-op: Choosing Your Approach

Windgate works either way, but the two styles demand different Windgate tools and different expectations. Solo runs are quieter and more flexible; group runs are faster and more forgiving of individual mistakes — provided someone is actually coordinating.

| Factor | Solo | Co-op |

## FAQ

**What are Windgate tools, exactly?**
There's no single official item called a "Windgate tool." In practice, the phrase refers to the loadout you assemble for a Windgate run: your primary and backup axes, a suitable vehicle, lighting for visibility, empty inventory space, and a small cash reserve. Think of it as a job-specific toolkit rather than a purchasable item.

**Do I need a specific axe for Windgate?**
Community reports don't point to one mandatory axe. What matters more is cutting efficiency and reliability — bring your fastest dependable cutter and a backup. If you're still working through the early axe progression, spend a few sessions building income first; a stronger general-purpose axe makes every Windgate trip easier.

**Is Windgate worth doing if I'm early in the game?**
Usually not as a first priority. Because it plays like a mid-to-late-game milestone, the return on investment is much better once you have steady income and solid map knowledge. Early players get more value from nearby groves, then graduate to longer trips.
