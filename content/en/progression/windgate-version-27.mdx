## How Windgate Funding Progresses, Step by Step

The word "progression" matters here. Grantmaking is a process with stages, and understanding those stages is far more useful than chasing a version number that does not exist.

Most private arts foundations move through a recognizable arc. Windgate's own published guidelines remain the authoritative source for its specific process, but the general sequence looks like this.

| Stage | What happens | What you should have ready |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Alignment check | Confirm your work fits the funder's stated focus | A one-paragraph program summary |
| 2. Initial contact | Letter of inquiry or invitation, if accepted | Budget range, timeline, expected outcomes |
| 3. Full proposal | Detailed narrative, budget, and leadership review | Financial statements and board information |
| 4. Evaluation | Staff review, follow-up questions, sometimes a site visit | Clear metrics and an honest reporting plan |
| 5. Award | Funds released with reporting requirements attached | A schedule for updates and outcomes |
| 6. Renewal or growth | Continued support based on demonstrated results | Documented impact and a next-phase plan |

A few practical notes for anyone navigating this path:

- **Read the guidelines before anything else.** Many private foundations do not accept unsolicited proposals, and sending a full proposal uninvited wastes everyone's time.
- **Match the request to the funder's language.** If an organization funds facilities and residencies, a request for general operating support for a traveling exhibit is a poor fit.
- **Build the relationship early.** Program staff are the people who explain what a funder is actually looking for.
- **Document outcomes continuously.** Reporting is not a formality; it is how renewal conversations start.
- **Be patient with capital projects.** Buildings and studios move on construction timelines, not grant cycles.

Community reports from arts-education circles consistently suggest the same thing: the organizations that succeed with private funders are the ones that treat the process as a multi-year relationship rather than a single application.
